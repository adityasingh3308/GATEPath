1. Overview

GATEPath is a learning platform designed to help students prepare for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE). We aim to handle personal information responsibly and explain our practices clearly.

This policy describes the information that may be collected through your account and your use of GATEPath, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

02

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use and how the platform is configured, GATEPath may collect the following:

Account information

Details such as your name, email address, and authentication information required to create and manage your account.

Profile information

Optional or requested academic details, such as college, degree, branch, graduation year, and GATE paper preferences.

Learning activity

Practice responses, scores, mock-test results, and progress information when those features record your activity.

Technical information

Limited technical or diagnostic information may be processed to operate, maintain, and secure the platform, depending on the server configuration.

The exact information collected depends on the features and integrations enabled on GATEPath.

03

How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Register users and manage account access.
  • Display profile details and saved preferences.
  • Provide practice questions, mock tests, and learning progress.
  • Maintain platform functionality and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Protect accounts and help prevent misuse of the platform.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations where required.
04

Account security

GATEPath is designed to restrict account-specific features to authenticated users where required. You should keep your login credentials confidential and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access.

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. However, no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Never send your password, verification codes, or other login secrets through the Contact Us form.

05

Cookies and preferences

GATEPath may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to support essential functionality and remember settings such as your theme preference.

The use of these technologies depends on the application's actual implementation. You can review your browser settings to manage or clear locally stored site data, though some features may then behave differently.

06

Data sharing

GATEPath does not intend to make your private account information publicly visible through ordinary learning features.

Information may be accessed by authorised service providers or disclosed where necessary to operate the platform, protect its security, comply with applicable law, or respond to valid legal requests. Any actual third-party integrations should be reflected in this policy.

07

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain learning records, resolve disputes, protect platform security, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Specific retention periods and account deletion procedures depend on the features implemented by GATEPath. Contact us if you have a question about your information.

08

Your choices

You can review and update profile information through the account features available on GATEPath. You may also contact us with questions about your personal information or a request relating to it.

Requests may need to be verified before action is taken. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

09

Children's privacy

GATEPath is an educational platform. If you are under the age applicable to independent consent under local law, please use the platform with appropriate parent or guardian involvement where required. We do not intend to collect children's personal information unlawfully.

10

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as GATEPath develops new features, changes its data practices, or responds to legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
