THE IDEA

One project. One learning goal.

GATEPath brings exam preparation resources together in one place, helping students study, practise, and review their progress through a structured experience.

Structured preparation

Organised syllabus information and preparation guidance to help learners plan their studies.

Practice and assessment

Practice questions and mock-test features designed to support exam readiness.

Continuous improvement

An evolving learning platform built with a focus on usability, clarity, and useful feedback.
