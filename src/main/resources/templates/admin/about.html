WHO WE ARE

Preparation with a clearer direction.

Preparing for GATE involves understanding concepts, covering the syllabus, solving questions and evaluating your readiness. Keeping these activities organised can make the preparation process easier to manage.

GATEPath brings these steps together in one place, helping learners navigate their preparation journey through structured resources and practice tools.

OUR MISSION

Make every study session count.

Our aim is to provide a structured preparation experience that helps students identify what to study, practise what they learn and understand where they can improve.
