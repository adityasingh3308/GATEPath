General Instructions

  1. This mock test contains 65 questions and carries 100 marks.
  2. The total duration is 180 minutes. The timer starts when you begin the test.
  3. The paper is divided into General Aptitude (10 questions) and Core CSE (55 questions).
  4. Questions may be MCQ, MSQ, or Numerical Answer Type (NAT).
  5. Use the question palette to navigate between questions.
  6. You can save an answer, clear a response, or mark a question for review.
  7. Your answers are saved as you navigate through the test.
  8. When the timer expires, the test will be submitted automatically.
  9. You can attempt this mock test a maximum of 2 times.
